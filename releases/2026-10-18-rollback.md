## Known-wrong facts this release publishes

The operator's words: "roll back with errors, then we'll fix just the minor errors". These are the errors, checked against the built pages, and they are the fix patch's checklist. Six stale strings, grouped by the fix they need.

1. **The retired mailing address.** `P.O. Box 2154` appears on `contact.html` and `donate.html`. The campaign's address became 15 Cottage Road, Suite 2154 on 2026-11-23. `get-involved.html` carries a different box, the Portland Press Herald's letters address, so it is not on this list.
2. **The October 27 workshop as an invitation.** The quoted invitation ("Make plans to come to the City Council workshop on Tuesday, Oct. 27 at 6 p.m., South Portland High School") sits on `index.html` and `act-now.html`, and a ticker reading "Next: City Council workshop - Tuesday, Oct. 27" runs on 16 pages. That date has passed.
3. **"12 times" a standard.** `index.html`, `act-now.html` and `tank-farms.html` say a reading was twelve times a standard.
4. **"State standards."** The same three pages plus `our-campaigns.html` say the readings exceeded state standards. Maine publishes ambient air guidelines, and the corrected framing is in the withdrawn 2026-11-24 batch.
5. **"Twitter" on `contact.html`**, where the rest of the site has said X since 2026-11-23.
6. **The October 15 community meeting as an invitation.** The "Now is the time to act" block on `index.html` opens "Join neighbors on Thursday, Oct. 15 for a community meeting to share ideas, concerns, and stories", where the site's current tree says neighbors met that day. It is a separate line from the October 27 workshop in item 2, and it sits earlier in the same block.

There is no "sign the petition" line in this footer; the original report guessed one and corrected itself. Nothing else in the build reads as stale to the production lane's check.

Both stale asks sit in the block whose whole point is being current, one about October 15 and one about October 27. The fix patch should therefore rewrite that block from the communications document's December 1 material rather than patch two dates into October copy.
