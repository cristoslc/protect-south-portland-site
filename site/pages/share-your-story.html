Share your story

We want to hear your voice

We are building an archive of our shared history and hoped-for future, and we want to share it with decision-makers and with the public.

For the 85 years that tank farms have loomed over South Portland, we have questioned what their presence means for our safety. Thanks to community efforts to implement air quality monitoring, we now have much of the data we have long sought. We are about to turn to the next chapter.

On December 1, the City Council will hold a workshop to consider how to substantially reduce emissions and prevent harm to residents' health. In advance of that meeting, Protect South Portland invites you to submit a voice recording of your reflections, ideas, and joys.

How to share

Call our Google Voice number and leave us a message:

Call (207) 370-2160

Google Voice caps messages at three minutes. Shorter messages of one to two minutes are welcome. If you would like to share for longer, please call us more than once.

What you agree we can do with your recording

By leaving us a voicemail, you are giving Protect South Portland permission to share any part of your recording, along with your name and any address information you provide, publicly. This will allow us to compile portions of contributors' messages to tell our collective story. We will let you know, through the Protect South Portland newsletter, when and where they are shared.

Please begin with

  • Your name, however you want to be identified
  • Your address or neighborhood, in whatever level of detail is comfortable to you
  • Any additional context you feel is important

Then share whatever comes to mind

If you would like a place to start, pick whichever line below opens something up.

  • I remember…
  • A fond memory I hold here is…
  • … matters to me because…
  • I first became aware or involved when…
  • I wish I knew…
  • I want others to know…
  • I hope…
  • If we are wildly successful, in whatever way that means to you, living and working in South Portland would look and feel like…

An optional image

If you would like to include a photo or other visual artifact as part of your contribution, please email it, along with your name and any relevant details about your submission, to protectsouthportland@gmail.com.

This collection is meant to be a snapshot in time. We plan to offer additional voice memo invitations at key points in the future, so you can share more than once.

Questions? Email us at protectsouthportland@gmail.com. If you want to speak in person, the Dec. 1 workshop is the next chance: the date and details are on Act now.
