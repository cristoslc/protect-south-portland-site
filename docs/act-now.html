Email the City Council to stop tank farms from dumping hazardous pollutants

The City Council workshop was held on Oct. 27. The Council took no vote, and the options it asked for return at the workshop it plans for Tuesday, Dec. 1. Councilors still need to hear from residents.

Email the Council and ask it to require better technology at the tank farms, and to make public what special counsel advises about the city’s authority.

Email the Council

City Council email addresses

The City Council email addresses are found here: southportland.gov/411/City-Council. Please verify addresses before you write.

We recommend sending your email to the general email address (citycouncil@southportland.gov), as well as each councilor individually:

  • Carter Scott (District 1): cscott@southportland.gov
  • Rachael Coleman (District 2): rcoleman@southportland.gov. Ellen Clancy takes this seat at the Dec. 7 inauguration.
  • Misha Pride (District 3): mpride@southportland.gov
  • Jessica Walker (District 4): jwalker@southportland.gov
  • Elyse Tipton (Mayor — District 5): etipton@southportland.gov
  • Richard Matthews (At-Large): rmatthews@southportland.gov
  • Natalie West (At-Large): nwest@southportland.gov

A message you can send

Dear Councilors,

New state fence-line data from Maine’s petroleum terminals shows benzene above Maine’s ambient air guideline at ten of the eleven. Six of the eleven terminals are in South Portland, and all but one came in above the guideline. At South Portland Terminal on Lincoln Street, the long-term average ran as high as four and a half times the guideline.

I ask you to require better technology at the tank farms, and to make public what special counsel advises about the city’s authority. Our families should not be paying for the oil companies’ profits with our health.

Sincerely,
[Your name and street or neighborhood]

If you are interested in having your voice heard in person, public comment on any subject is allowed for up to 3 minutes at all City Council meetings. Find schedules and agendas on the City’s agendas portal.

The Council plans its next workshop for Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 6:30 p.m. in City Hall Council Chambers, 25 Cottage Road. Arrive by 6:15 to fill the room. To get an email when the agenda is posted, write to protectsouthportland@gmail.com with “Workshop alerts” in the subject line.
