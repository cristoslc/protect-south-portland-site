Example message

Dear Councilors,

New state fence-line data from Maine’s petroleum terminals shows benzene above Maine’s ambient air guideline at ten of the eleven. Six of the eleven terminals are in South Portland, and all but one came in above the guideline. At South Portland Terminal on Lincoln Street, the long-term average ran as high as four and a half times the guideline.

I ask you to require better technology at the tank farms, and to make public what special counsel advises about the city’s authority. Our families should not be paying for the oil companies’ profits with our health.

Sincerely,
[Your name and street or neighborhood]

Writing a letter to the editor

How to write a letter: the toolkit’s letter-to-the-editor guidance.

Sentry

Email your letter to letters@metln.org or use the letter submission form. Letters should be no longer than 350 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and phone number. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Forecaster

Email your letter to letters@metln.org or use the letter submission form. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and must include the writer’s name, full address, and daytime and evening phone numbers. The deadline is Monday at noon.

Portland Press Herald

Email your letter to letterstotheeditor@pressherald.com or use the letter submission form, or send mail to Editor, Portland Press Herald, P.O. Box 1460, Portland, ME 04104-5009. Letters should be no longer than 300 words and include the writer’s name, address, and daytime phone number.
