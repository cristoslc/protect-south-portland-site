Our work

Protect South Portland’s focus is returning to the tanks and the emissions: what residents at the fence line have felt and smelled for years, and what the new fence-line data now confirms.

South Portland Tank Farms

120 large petroleum tanks run by six major companies. State fence-line monitoring finds benzene above Maine’s ambient air guideline at ten of the state’s eleven petroleum terminals, and at the South Portland Terminal as high as four and a half times the guideline. See the interactive map, the fence-line readings and where the campaign stands, all on one page.

Coastal flooding

Storm surge floods our streets and working waterfront. PSP works with the City’s coastal resilience efforts and tracks what flooding means for the tank farms.
