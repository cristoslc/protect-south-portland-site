The Dec. 1 Council workshop has a new venue

The City Council’s next tank-farms workshop has moved from City Hall to the South Portland High School Lecture Hall, 637 Highland Ave. Same date and time: Tuesday, Dec. 1 at 6:30 p.m. Wear green, bring your neighbors, and arrive by 6:15 to fill the room.

Tuesday, Dec. 1 — City Council workshop on the tank farms

  • What: a public workshop where staff and special counsel deliver their report on the tank farms, and the Council weighs what action to take.
  • When: Tuesday, Dec. 1, 6:30 p.m. Arrive by 6:15 to fill the room.
  • Where: South Portland High School Lecture Hall, 637 Highland Ave, South Portland, ME
  • Wear: green!

Get directions to 637 Highland Ave Email the Council first

The meeting moved — the ask is the same

The Council originally planned this workshop for the City Hall Council Chambers at 25 Cottage Road. The meeting has moved to the high school’s Lecture Hall on Highland Ave; the date and time are unchanged. Wear green, bring your neighbors, and come early.

The agenda

The city posts the Council’s agenda for each meeting. You can look for the Dec. 1 agenda here:

  • BoardDocs (city meeting packets)
  • City Agenda Center
  • City Council calendar

Under the Council’s standing rules (PDF), City Council agenda packets must be published by 4:30 p.m. on the Friday before the meeting week. We’ll share the agenda when it posts; to get an email when it does, write to protectsouthportland@gmail.com with “Workshop alerts” in the subject line.

Talking points

This is the workshop where the strongest action possible is on the table. A few points to raise:

  • Urge the Council to take the strongest action possible on the tank farms. The Council is considering what — if any — action to take, and staff and special counsel will be delivering a report at this workshop.
  • Ask the Council to require better technology at the tank farms, and to make public what special counsel advises about the city’s authority.
  • Remind the Council of its own record: South Portland set a precedent with its Clear Skies Ordinance, and the community expects that same resolve here.

Public comment

Members of the public have an opportunity to comment for up to 3 minutes each on an item on the agenda, with the limit able to be extended at the Council’s discretion. Workshop items are agenda items, so your three minutes at a workshop count.

You can also email the Council

If you can’t make the meeting — or want your message there before you go — use our ready-to-send message on the Act now page.
